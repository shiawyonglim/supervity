# 🎬 3-Minute Demo Recording Script

This script breaks down exactly what to show on your screen and what to say during the 3-minute video portion of your pitch. It ensures you explicitly demo all 6 required surfaces of the Command Center.

## 🛠️ Recording Setup
* **Tool**: Use Loom, OBS Studio, or your Mac/Windows built-in screen recorder.
* **Format**: Record this as one smooth 3-minute video (or stitch 3 short clips together). Do not try to run it live during the pitch to avoid internet/server crashes.
* **Tip**: Move your mouse purposefully to what you are talking about. Don't wave it around randomly.

---

## ⏱️ Clip 1: The Hub & The Chat (1 Minute)
**Surfaces Covered:** Dashboard, AI Manager
**What to show on screen:**
1. Open the **Dashboard**. Point out the live KPIs and activity charts showing leads flowing through successfully.
2. Switch to the **AI Manager** (Chat interface).
3. Type a query like: *"What is the status of the Acme Corp lead?"*
4. Show the AI Manager responding with live data straight from the database.

**What to say (Voiceover):**
> *"Welcome to the Command Center. We start at the **Dashboard**, giving revenue leaders a real-time, bird's-eye view of all automated pipeline activity. But visibility isn't just about charts. If a manager needs specific context, they can use our conversational **AI Manager**. I can simply ask it for the status of a specific account, and it queries the database in real-time, proving that our AI is deeply connected to our live operational data."*

---

## ⏱️ Clip 2: Data Management & The Safety Net (1 Minute)
**Surfaces Covered:** Data Manager, Workbench
**What to show on screen:**
1. Switch to the **Data Manager** (Routing or Deduplication tab).
2. Click "Run Routing Engine" or "Run Deduplication" and show a lead failing or overflowing capacity.
3. Switch immediately to the **Workbench**.
4. Click on the halted exception. Highlight the **AI Recommendation** and click **Resolve**.

**What to say (Voiceover):**
> *"Next, we manage the mess. Our **Data Manager** actively deduplicates records and routes leads based on live rep capacity. But what happens when the AI catches a privacy mismatch or a rep hits max capacity? The Orchestrator halts the pipeline instantly and sends it to our safety net: the **Workbench**. Here, human operators retain ultimate control over risky decisions. Notice the AI has already pre-calculated a resolution recommendation, making human-in-the-loop exception handling lightning fast."*

---

## ⏱️ Clip 3: Actionable Insights & Governed Policies (1 Minute)
**Surfaces Covered:** AI Insights, AI Policies
**What to show on screen:**
1. Switch to the **AI Insights** page.
2. Scroll to a specific action item (e.g., "Merge 'IBM' and 'Intl Business Machines'") and click the one-click Action button.
3. Switch to the **AI Policies** page.
4. Show the newly created policy rules sitting in the engine, strictly governing the AI's future behavior.

**What to say (Voiceover):**
> *"Finally, a true operation must get smarter over time. Our **AI Insights** engine doesn't just show static graphs; it analyzes how humans resolve exceptions in the Workbench and generates actionable recommendations. With one click, an administrator can turn an insight into a hard rule inside our **AI Policies** engine. This ensures that the AI learns from its mistakes, humans never do the same manual work twice, and the entire agentic workforce remains strictly governed."*
